APPLICATIONS

One architecture. Seven domains.

Different systems bring different dimensions and conflicts. These seven cases show the range of contexts in which a shared system-diagnostic approach can be applied.

01 / MATERIALS

Textile Pilling

How fibre, yarn, fabric structure and surface interact under abrasion and use.

02 / DIAGNOSTICS

Environmental Diagnostic Signals

How temperature, humidity, light and capillary transport can interact with a diagnostic signal.

03 / POWER SYSTEMS

Central Vietnam Power Grid

How weather conditions, electrical networks, protection systems and infrastructure dependencies intersect.

04 / WATER INFRASTRUCTURE

Water Infrastructure

How material condition, operating state and environmental pressures can affect infrastructure stability.

05 / ENERGY STORAGE

Lithium–Silicon Anodes

How volume change, mechanical integrity, interphase behaviour and ion transport interact.

06 / AEROSPACE

Flapping-Wing Micro Aircraft

How geometry, aerodynamics, mass limits and dynamic stability constrain a small flying system.

07 / EXPLORATION

Gold Exploration

How geological potential connects with capital, development, infrastructure and water requirements.

DOMAIN-AGNOSTIC BY DESIGN

Different domains. Different conflicts. A shared diagnostic approach.

The cases are not identical problems. Their purpose is to show that the system can examine distinct kinds of systems without being confined to one industry.
