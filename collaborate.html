An Open Invitation

Bring your system into focus.

Every system has relationships that are difficult to see from within a single perspective. New questions need new conversations across disciplines.

THE WHITE-BOX SYSTEM invites companies, technical leaders, researchers, specialists and curious problem-solvers to explore complex systems together — across sectors, functions and environmental conditions.

A GROWING SYSTEM LIBRARY

Different perspectives. Shared questions.

Bring a system, a challenge or an unresolved question. Together, we can examine relevant functions, processes, dependencies and environmental conditions — and make potential conflicts easier to identify and investigate.

01 / BRING

Bring your question

Describe the system you want to understand, the situation you are facing and the question that matters to you.

02 / EXAMINE

Explore the relationships

Combine system-level structure with the knowledge of people who understand the domain. Map relevant interactions, dependencies and potential conflicts.

03 / EVALUATE

Build understanding

Review available information, distinguish evidence from assumptions and identify what can be evaluated, validated or still needs investigation.

WHO CAN TAKE PART?

Be the first in your field.

Whether you work in engineering, manufacturing, research, biology, infrastructure, finance or a field not yet represented, your system may open a valuable new line of inquiry.

You bring your expertise and the question. I bring a structured approach to mapping system relationships and tracing the path towards an assessment.

OPEN TO PARTNERSHIPS

Start with a conversation.

Initial conversations help establish the question, scope and a suitable way to work together. A case may inform further evaluation or the growing system library, but inclusion or publication is never automatic.

Confidentiality matters. Do not send sensitive or proprietary information before scope and confidentiality arrangements have been agreed. Non-disclosure agreements (NDAs) are signed before confidential information is exchanged.
