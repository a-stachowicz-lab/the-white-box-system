Bring us your system.

A useful first enquiry does not need to contain every detail. It should establish the system boundary and the decision or uncertainty you want to examine.

  1. Your system
    Company, project, material, process or infrastructure.
  2. Your key question
    What needs to be understood, checked or resolved?
  3. Your available information
    Reports, technical documents, data, diagrams or public sources.

WHAT HAPPENS NEXT

Define the scope first.

The initial step is to clarify the question, the information available and the boundaries of the requested diagnosis. Scope, deliverables, timing and commercial terms should be agreed before work begins.
