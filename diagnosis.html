System Diagnosis

A structured system diagnosis.

A structured diagnosis examines your system, maps relevant conflicts and dependencies, and separates evidence-supported findings from open questions.

THE SERVICE

From a system question to a traceable assessment.

The scope is defined around your actual question. The work then examines the information supplied and identifies relevant system relationships and conflicts.

01 / INPUT

Define the system

Describe the company, project, material, process or infrastructure under consideration.

02 / ANALYSIS

Map conflicts

Organise relevant constraints, dependencies and interactions within the agreed scope.

03 / OUTPUT

Document findings

Provide a traceable assessment that distinguishes findings, assumptions and information gaps.

WHAT TO PREPARE

Three starting points.

  1. Your system — company, project or process.
  2. Your key question — what needs to be understood or resolved?
  3. Your available information — reports, technical documents, data or public sources.

SCOPE AND LIMITS

Evidence-led, not a promise of a predetermined result.

The findings depend on the agreed scope and the quality and completeness of the information available. Missing data and unresolved questions are identified rather than concealed.

Confidentiality: If a diagnosis requires confidential or non-public information, a Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) can be agreed and signed before that information is shared. The scope and terms must be agreed by both parties.
