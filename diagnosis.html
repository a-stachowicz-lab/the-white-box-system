SYSTEM DIAGNOSIS

Start with the question.
Map what matters.

A focused diagnosis begins with a clear system boundary, a question worth answering and a transparent view of the evidence available.

01 / YOUR SYSTEM

What are we examining?

Describe the company, project, material, infrastructure, process or other system you want to understand.

02 / YOUR KEY QUESTION

What needs to be understood?

State the decision, uncertainty, conflict or dependency you want to investigate.

03 / AVAILABLE INFORMATION

What evidence exists?

Reports, technical documents, datasets, diagrams, observations or relevant public sources can define the starting point.

EXPECTED OUTPUT

A bounded, traceable analysis

The scope is defined, relevant conflicts and dependencies are mapped, and conclusions are limited to what the available evidence supports. Missing information is identified explicitly.
