Galvanic Matrix

Galvanic conflict assessment.

The Galvanic Matrix is a structured assessment component for identifying potential electrochemical incompatibilities under defined conditions.

PURPOSE

Identify potential incompatibilities.

Material pairings can interact differently depending on their electrochemical properties and the conditions of use. A structured assessment helps identify combinations that require closer examination.

CONTEXT

Conditions matter.

Environmental conditions and contact configurations can affect the assessment. Case-specific engineering decisions require appropriate input data and validation.
