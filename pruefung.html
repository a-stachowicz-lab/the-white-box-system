AI OUTPUT REVIEW

When AI claims something no one can substantiate.

I review one AI output — a report, a proposal, or an analysis — and identify which claims are supported, which are fabricated, and which remain open. You receive two pages: findings, gaps, and sources.

Request a review How it works

THE RESULT

Two pages. Three parts.

01

Findings

Which claims hold up — and what supports them. Every figure with its origin.

02

Gaps

What is missing to substantiate a claim. Specified by document and responsible party — no vague “data unclear.”

03

Sources

Where each value comes from, with confidence noted. What cannot be substantiated remains open — and is explicitly marked as such.

THE PROCESS

Three steps. Three to five business days.

1

Intake

Sie schicken die Ausgabe. Ich lese sie vollständig — und markiere jede Aussage, die auf einer Zahl, einer Norm oder einer Sources beruht.

2

Review

Each claim is classified as supported, derived, or open. Derived information is labelled as such, never presented as a measured value.

3

Findings

Two pages and a 30-minute discussion. No recommendation that the evidence cannot support.

SCOPE AND COST

Turnaround: three to five business days after receipt of the complete output.

Price: erste Review kostenlos. Weitere Reviewen und laufende Begleitung nach Umfang — Festpreis, keine Stundenabrechnung.

THE LIMITS

Was diese Review nicht ist.

No value judgment

I do not judge whether an investment is wise or a product is good. I check whether the claims can be substantiated.

No implementation

I do not redesign your processes or rewrite your reports. I identify what holds up and what does not.

WHO IT IS FOR

Quality and development

Management
