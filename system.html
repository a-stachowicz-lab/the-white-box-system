System Architecture

Understand the system as a whole.

The White-Box System examines how a system's dimensions, conditions and interactions shape its current state.

A SHARED DIAGNOSTIC ARCHITECTURE

Different systems. Relevant dimensions. Connected conflicts.

The starting point is the system itself. Its relevant dimensions may include geometry, materials, processes, environment, resources and operating conditions. The specific dimensions depend on the system being examined.

01

Establish the system

Define the system boundary, its relevant dimensions and the conditions in which it operates.

02

Examine interactions

Map dependencies, constraints and conflicts across the relevant dimensions.

03

Trace the diagnosis

Make the path from the described state through identified conflicts to the assessment explainable.

KNOWLEDGE STRUCTURE

Make complex knowledge visible.

The system is designed to make relationships between information, constraints and evidence easier to examine and trace.

Domain-agnostic. System-specific.

The overarching architecture can be applied across domains. The parameters, system information and domain-specific conditions are selected for each individual case.
