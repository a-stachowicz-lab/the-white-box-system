WORKSHOP · HALF DAY

Make AI outputs checkable.

A practical workshop for small and medium-sized businesses. Work with AI outputs from your own day-to-day practice, learn to question claims and evidence, and develop a more conscious way to use the AI tools you already have.

Enquire about a date See how it works

WHO IT IS FOR

For businesses already using AI.

You do not need to know how AI is built. This workshop helps you use the tools already available to you more consciously, critically and confidently — at work and, where useful, at home.

Owners and managers

Build a clearer basis for handling AI-assisted material before it is sent, shared or relied on.

Quality and technical teams

Practise checking reports, evaluations and other outputs where sources, figures and statements matter.

Small teams

Agree on practical shared guidance instead of leaving everyone to work it out alone.

THREE DAYS BEFORE THE WORKSHOP

Your document. My preparation.

Each participant sends one anonymised AI-generated or AI-assisted document three days before the workshop. Please remove personal, confidential and commercially sensitive information.

YOUR PART

Send one real output

Choose a report, evaluation or other document created or assisted by an AI model you actually use. The document becomes the basis for your practical exercise.

MY PREPARATION

An individual two-page review

I review each submitted document in advance and prepare an individual, two-page review. You receive it during the workshop, as a reference for comparing your own findings with my review.

THE WORKSHOP · FOUR HOURS

First your own assessment. Then the evidence.

The workshop is built around practical work with real material. You begin by checking your own document without the cards, then repeat the review with the checking tools and compare your findings with my prior review.

01 · FIRST REVIEW

Look at the output yourself

Review your own submitted document without the cards. Identify statements that may need checking, unsupported claims, missing sources or unanswered questions. Record your findings on your own one-page results sheet.

02 · THE CHECKING CARDS

Use the seven rules and four evidence classes

Repeat and deepen your review with the cards. Learn to distinguish supported statements, derived information and open questions, and to make the basis for your assessment explicit.

03 · COMPARE THE REVIEWS

See what you found — and what you missed

Compare your one-page results sheet with my prepared two-page review. Discuss which issues you identified, which were overlooked and where evidence is still missing. The aim is to strengthen your own ability to check AI outputs.

04 · YOUR EVERYDAY AI

Work with the models you already use

We look at how you use your usual AI tools, including on a smartphone. Where relevant, we examine available settings together and practise applying the checking approach to your own everyday tasks.

05 · YOUR WORKPLACE

Develop practical shared guidance

Adapt the approach to your team's actual work. Agree on usable rules for checking outputs, handling missing information and deciding when an answer needs further verification.

06 · APPLY IT AFTERWARDS

Take the tools with you

Use the cards, checklist and agreed guidance after the workshop — at work and, where useful, in your personal use of AI.

WHAT YOU TAKE AWAY

Your results and practical tools.

The workshop gives you both a personal exercise result and a prepared review of your own submitted document.

YOUR OWN WORK

Your own findings from the review exercise, recorded before the group compares them with the prepared review.

MY PREPARATION

Individual two-page review

My review of your submitted AI output, prepared before the workshop and used as a comparison reference during the session.

Seven Rules card

A practical reminder for checking AI outputs before using or sharing them.

Evidence Classes card

Review checklist

Five questions to ask before passing an output on. The final question: “Would I sign my name to this?”

Your one-page rulebook

Practical shared guidance adapted to your team's work and responsibilities.
