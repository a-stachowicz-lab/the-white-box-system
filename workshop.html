WORKSHOP · HALF DAY

Make AI outputs checkable.

A practical workshop for small and medium-sized businesses. Build confidence in evaluating AI-generated information, using the tools already available to you and making informed decisions about what to trust.

Enquire about a date Explore the workshop

WHO IT IS FOR

For businesses already using AI.

You do not need to know how AI is built. This workshop helps you use the tools already available to you more consciously, critically and confidently — at work and, where useful, at home.

Owners and managers

Build a clearer basis for handling AI-assisted work and decisions.

Teams and specialists

Strengthen confidence when working with reports, information and AI-supported tasks.

Small businesses

Develop a shared approach to using AI responsibly in everyday work.

BEFORE THE WORKSHOP

Your work. My preparation.

Each participant submits one anonymised AI-generated or AI-assisted document three days before the workshop. Please remove personal, confidential and commercially sensitive information.

YOUR CONTRIBUTION

Bring a real example

Choose a report, evaluation or other document created or assisted by an AI model you actually use. It will provide a practical starting point for the workshop.

INDIVIDUAL PREPARATION

A review prepared in advance

I examine each submitted document before the session and prepare an individual review. You will use it during the workshop as a reference for reflecting on your own assessment.

THE WORKSHOP · FOUR HOURS

Practical knowledge for a more confident use of AI.

We work with real examples and everyday situations. The focus is on building understanding, judgement and practical confidence — without requiring technical or programming knowledge.

01 · UNDERSTANDING AI OUTPUTS

Evaluate information with confidence

Develop a more considered approach to AI-generated content and the information it presents.

02 · INFORMATION AND EVIDENCE

Recognise reliability and uncertainty

Explore how to assess information, sources and statements before relying on or sharing them.

03 · AI IN EVERYDAY WORK

Use the tools you already have

Reflect on your current use of AI and identify ways to work with it more deliberately in everyday tasks.

04 · RESOURCE AWARENESS

What AI carries in its ecological backpack

Take a closer look at the physical resources behind AI and what conscious, efficient use can mean.

05 · RESPONSIBLE USE

Build shared guidance for your workplace

Develop a practical understanding of responsible AI use that reflects your team's work and needs.

06 · PERSONAL CONFIDENCE

Make informed decisions

Strengthen your ability to question, assess and use AI outputs independently — at work and beyond.
