WORKSHOP · HALF DAY

Make AI outputs checkable.

Four practical hours with your own AI-generated outputs. You will practise checking claims, work with seven simple rules and four evidence classes, and develop shared guidance that fits your day-to-day work.

WHO IT IS FOR

For businesses already using AI.

AI is already part of everyday work in many businesses — sometimes without shared guidance. This workshop is not a technical course about how AI is built. It helps people use the tools they already have more consciously, critically and confidently.

Owners and managers

Build a clearer basis for handling AI-assisted material before it is sent, shared or relied on.

Quality and technical teams

Practise checking reports, evaluations and other outputs where sources and figures matter.

Small teams

Agree on practical shared guidance, rather than leaving everyone to work it out alone.

THE WORKSHOP

Learn by checking real material.

Participants work with their own anonymised AI outputs. They start without the cards, then repeat the review using the rules and evidence classes. The difference becomes visible through practice, not just explanation.

01 · FIRST REVIEW

Look at the output yourself

Start with a few real examples of errors. Then review your own output and identify claims that may not be supported.

02 · THE CARDS

Use the seven rules

Bring structure to what you found. The seven rules and four evidence classes help distinguish supported statements, derived information and open questions.

03 · YOUR WORKPLACE

Agree on shared guidance

Discuss what fits your work: who checks what, what to do when information is missing, and how to handle gaps without guessing.

04 · TAKE IT WITH YOU

Make it usable after the workshop

Leave with practical cards, a checklist and a one-page rulebook adapted to your business — a reference for everyday use at work and at home.

WHAT YOU TAKE AWAY

Practical tools that stay with you.

Seven Rules card

A desk-side reminder for checking AI outputs before using or sharing them.

Evidence Classes card

E1–E4: primary evidence, independent specialist sources, derived information and open hypotheses — with the differences made clear.

Review checklist

Five questions to ask before passing an output on. The final question: “Would I sign my name to this?”

Your one-page rulebook

A shared set of practical rules adapted to your team's work and responsibilities.
